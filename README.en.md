## Why it exists

The 2011 Harvard Business Review study and Drift measured the minute-by-minute decay curve
of lead conversion: answer late and the contact is gone. The median first response among
Italian SMBs is **around 277 minutes** (Drift). Within those hours, the prospect has
already written to someone else.

agentemIA brings the first response down to **real time** — chat, phone and booking —
without hiring anyone. A real employee costs €1,500–2,000 a month in Italy.
The AI department starts at €39.

The landing page has a [calculator](https://agentemia.it/#roi): enter three of your numbers
and see how much revenue slips away every year.
